# TechStore Policies

## 1. Warranty Policy
- All smartphones and laptops come with a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase. Accessories come with a 6-month warranty (or 12 months where stated on the product page).
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects only. It does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, accidental drops, or damage caused by unauthorized repairs.
- To claim warranty, bring the product along with the original bill and the warranty card to any TechStore outlet or contact our support line.
- During the warranty period, repairs and replacement parts are provided free of charge at our authorized service centre.
- Warranty claims are typically processed within 5-7 working days.

## 2. Replacement Policy (7-Day Replacement)
- If you receive a product that is defective, dead on arrival, or has a manufacturing defect, you can request a replacement within 7 days of purchase.
- Replacement is subject to verification at our store. The product must be in its original condition with all accessories, box, and bill.
- Replacement requests must be accompanied by the original purchase bill.
- After verification, a fresh unit is replaced immediately if stock is available, otherwise within 7 working days.

## 3. Return & Refund Policy
- We do not offer refunds on products that are in working condition, as all sales are final once the product is verified and billed.
- Refunds are only issued when a replacement unit is not available and the product is confirmed defective by our service centre.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7-10 working days after approval.
- If a product is purchased with a gift card or voucher, the refund is issued as store credit.

## 4. Exchange Policy
- We offer a buyback and exchange program: you can exchange your old phone or laptop for credit toward a new purchase.
- The exchange value is determined by the condition, model, and age of the old device after in-store inspection.
- Exchange offers cannot be combined with other discounts unless specified.
- Once exchanged, the old device cannot be taken back.

## 5. Delivery Policy
- We offer free delivery within the city for orders above Rs. 5,000. Orders below this amount incur a flat delivery fee of Rs. 99.
- Standard delivery takes 2-4 working days. Express delivery (same day or next day) is available for an additional fee.
- High-value items (phones and laptops) are only delivered after OTP verification and are not left unattended.
- Delivery is available in select cities. Please confirm your pincode with our team before ordering.

## 6. Payment Methods
- We accept Cash, all major Debit and Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and Net Banking.
- EMI options are available on all phones and laptops above Rs. 20,000 with select banks (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis).
- EMI is available for 3, 6, 9, or 12 months. No-cost EMI is available on select models during promotional periods.
- Payment is required in full before delivery for online orders.

## 7. Repair Services
- We provide in-store and doorstep repair services for phones and laptops.
- Common repairs: screen replacement, battery replacement, charging port repair, speaker and microphone fixes, software issues, and data recovery.
- Screen replacement for popular models is typically completed within 48 hours. Battery replacement within 24 hours.
- All repairs come with a 30-day service warranty on the repaired part.
- Repair diagnosis is free. Repair charges are quoted and approved by the customer before any work begins.

## 8. Price Match Policy
- If you find an identical product (same model, same storage variant) at a lower price at a registered store in the same city, we will match the price.
- The price must be verified by our team, either through a printed bill or a live online listing on a major platform.
- Price match is applicable at the time of purchase only and cannot be claimed retroactively.

## 9. Student Discount
- Students get an additional 5% discount on all laptops on presentation of a valid student ID.
- The student discount can be combined with bank offers but cannot be combined with exchange offers.
- Valid for in-store purchases only.

## 10. Bulk & Corporate Orders
- We offer special pricing for bulk orders (5 or more units) and corporate purchases.
- Corporate billing and GST invoicing are available for registered businesses.
- For bulk enquiries, please contact our corporate desk at corporate@techstore.example.com.

## 11. Gift Purchases
- Gift purchases can be returned or exchanged only if the product is defective and within the 7-day replacement window.
- Refunds for gifts are issued as store credit to the purchaser.
- Gift wrapping is available free of charge in store.

## 12. Damaged or Wrong Item
- If you receive a damaged item or a wrong item in an online delivery, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
- We will arrange a free pickup and ship the correct unit within 2-3 working days.
- If a replacement is not available, a full refund is processed within 7 working days.

## 13. Pre-Order Policy
- Pre-orders require a token amount of Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 5,000 depending on the product.
- The token is fully adjustable against the final price and is fully refundable if the product is cancelled before launch.
- Pre-orders are fulfilled in the order they were placed.

## 14. Privacy Policy
- We collect only the information needed to process your purchase and support requests.
- Your data is never sold to third parties.
- Payment details are processed through secure, PCI-compliant gateways.
