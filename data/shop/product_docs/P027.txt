Frequently Asked Questions
What are the key features of Galaxy A36 5G?
Galaxy A36 5G features a sleek, 17.01 cm FHD+ Super AMOLED display with a durable glass back and an IP67 rating, ensuring resistance to dust and water. With options for 6 GB, 8 GB or 12 GB Memory and 128 GB or 256 GB Storage, you'll have ample space to snap your best shots and store all your favorite photos and videos. Rest easy knowing your files and sensitive data are secure with EAL5+ certified Samsung Knox Vault, paired with 6 generations of OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates.
What makes Galaxy A36 5G ideal for social media and entertainment?
Galaxy A36 5G is the ideal companion for gaming, social media and entertainment. Powered by an Octa-core processor with a 15% larger vapor chamber and 5,000 mAh battery, Galaxy A36 5G offers smoother gameplay and long-lasting performance. Enjoy crisp, fluid visuals with a 120 Hz refresh rate and 1,200 nits brightness, ideal for streaming and watching videos. Capture stunning selfies with the 12 MP front camera and Video HDR, remove unwanted objects with Object Eraser and enhance your photos with one-of-a-kind Filters.
What are the differences between Galaxy A36 5G and Galaxy A35 5G?
The main difference between Galaxy A36 5G and Galaxy A35 5G is that Galaxy A36 5G has a larger 17.01 cm FHD+ Super AMOLED display with a higher peak brightness of 1,200 nits, slimmer bezels and a sleeker design. In contrast, Galaxy A35 5G features a 16.83 cm FHD+ Super AMOLED display with a peak brightness of 1,000 nits. Galaxy A36 5G also offers a 12 MP front camera with video HDR, 45 W for faster charging, 6 generations of OS and 6 years of security updates, versus the 13 MP front camera of the Galaxy A35 5G which supports 25 W charging, 4 generations of OS and 5 years of security updates.
How does Galaxy A36 5G work with the Galaxy Ecosystem?
Improve your experience by connecting and controlling your devices with ease. Simply pair your Galaxy A36 5G with Galaxy Fit3 to track your fitness, and connect to your Galaxy Buds FE to listen to your favorite tunes. Then, manage your devices seamlessly within the Galaxy Ecosystem.
