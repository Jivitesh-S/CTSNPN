Frequently Asked Questions
What are the key features of Galaxy Buds4?
Galaxy Buds4 supports everyday listening. Enhanced sound features include Samsung Seamless Codec (SSC) supporting up to 24-bit / 96 kHz, Adjust to fit option that adjusts to ear shape and wearing style and Super Clear Call making calls clear, even in noise. In terms of design, the new metal blade adds a sophisticated look to Galaxy Buds4. With a translucent cover, Charging Case is compact for portability. Connectivity has also been improved with seamless pairing available on Galaxy devices running on One UI 8.5. Easily control from settings and Quick Panel without a separate app. 16
What are the differences between Galaxy Buds4 Pro and Galaxy Buds4?
The main differences between Galaxy Buds4 Pro and Galaxy Bud4 are as follows. Galaxy Buds4 Pro has an enhanced 2-way Speaker and a Dual Amplifier to reproduce Hi-Fi sound that's true to the original recording. And it comes with 3 sets of silicone ear tips in different sizes for sound delivery with less distortion. Features such as Voice Detect and Siren Detect are supported on Galaxy Buds4 Pro. Galaxy Buds4 produces balanced sound with a capable speaker and is optimized for everyday listening. 17
What comes in the box with Galaxy Buds4?
Galaxy Buds4 comes with earbuds, Charging Case and the Quick Start Guide. USB Type-C cable is not included.
What colours does Galaxy Buds4 come in?
Galaxy Buds4 comes in two classic colours, White and Black. With timeless colours that never go out of style, Galaxy Buds4 blends into your outfit for any occasion.
Does the Charging Case support wireless charging?
Yes, Galaxy Buds4 Charging Case can be charged wirelessly. Wireless charging pad is not included in the box and is sold separately.
Does Galaxy Buds4 Pro and Galaxy Buds4 use the same Charging Case?
No. The Charging Case for each Buds is shaped differently, making it incompatible between the two models.
How does Pinch Control work?
You can use Pinch Control on Galaxy Buds4 using your index finger. First, wear the earbuds and try the following actions. Pinch once (Single pinch) to play or pause the track, answer or end a call and stop using features such as Read Notifications Aloud. Pinch twice (Double pinch) to play the next track. Pinch three times (Triple pinch) to play the previous track. Pinch and hold to turn on the preset feature or decline a call. Swipe up or down using your index finger to adjust the volume.
What is Samsung Seamless Codec (SSC) Ultra High Quality Audio (UHQ) Option?
You can turn on SSC - UHQ option when Galaxy Buds4 Series is paired with an eligible Galaxy device. The audio is delivered in extended 24-bit bit depth and 96 kHz sampling rate. Hear Hi-Fi sound in full detail, with rich audio information. 18
Which Galaxy smartphones and Galaxy Tab support SSC - UHQ?
SSC - UHQ supports up to 24-bit / 96 kHz and is available on Galaxy smartphones such as Galaxy S26 Series, S25 Series, S24 Series, S23 Series, Z Fold7, Z Flip7, Z Fold6, Z Flip6, Z Fold5 and Z Flip5. It's also available on Galaxy Tab S11 Series, Tab S10 Series and Tab S9 Series with One UI 6.1.1 or above. More devices may be supported in the future. 19
What is Super Clear Call?
Galaxy Buds4 Series isolates your voice input through an advanced Deep Neural Network (DNN) technology. The conversation between the caller and receiver is delivered through Super Wideband (SWB) up to 16 kHz, making the call crystal clear. 20
How do I connect Galaxy Buds4 to my Galaxy device?
Just open the Charging Case and you’re all set to connect Galaxy Buds4 to your Galaxy device. The earbuds automatically start pairing and a popup screen will appear on the Galaxy device. Tap Connect and the pairing is complete. 21
Do I need a separate app to control Galaxy Buds4?
You can control Galaxy Buds4 based on the version of One UI on your Galaxy device. On Galaxy devices running on One UI 8.0 or later, certain features can be controlled from the Quick Panel or you can manage Galaxy Buds4 in more detail from your phone's device settings without downloading any additional apps. For Galaxy devices running on earlier versions of One UI, configurations can be found in the Galaxy Wearable app. 22
What are the benefits of using Interpreter feature with Galaxy Buds4?
Your speech is delivered to Samsung Galaxy smartphone to be interpreted and played through its speakers. The opponent's speech is delivered to Samsung Galaxy smartphone to be interpreted but will be played through Galaxy Buds4 Series. Keep your earbuds on, even when you're talking with a person that speaks in another language. 23
What are the benefits of using Auto Switch on Galaxy Buds4?
Auto Switch uses Bluetooth to automatically deliver the audio source between the devices. The feature allows you to take incoming calls while you're working on a creative project on your Galaxy Tab or Galaxy Book. Once the call is over, you can go right back to your project effortlessly. 24
