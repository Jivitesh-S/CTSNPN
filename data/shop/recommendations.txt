# GENERAL ADVICE

## Phone vs laptop for students - which to buy first?
- If you need it for typing assignments, coding, or presentations: laptop first (Samsung Galaxy Book3 or Galaxy Book4).
- If you need portability, communication, and entertainment: phone first (Samsung Galaxy M35 5G or Galaxy A16 5G).
- Ideal combo under Rs. 70,000 total: a Rs. 18,999 phone (M35 5G) + a Rs. 47,990 laptop (Galaxy Book3).

## How much storage do I need?
- Phones: 128GB for most users, 256GB for photographers and gamers, 512GB+ only for heavy creators.
- Laptops: 512GB SSD minimum in 2026, 1TB if you edit video or game. An external SSD is the cheapest upgrade.

## How much RAM do I need?
- Phones: 6GB minimum, 8GB for gaming/multitasking, 12GB+ for power users.
- Laptops: 16GB is the sweet spot for students and professionals. 8GB is workable for light browsing, 32GB for creators.

## Galaxy S series or A series - which is right for me?
- Choose the S series (S25, S24, S23 FE) if: you want flagship performance, premium cameras, and 7 years of updates.
- Choose the A series (A55, A35, A26, A25) if: you want great value, expandable storage, and 5-6 years of updates for less.
- Advice: below Rs. 40,000 the A series gives far better value; above Rs. 80,000 the S series is excellent.

## Should I buy an extended warranty?
- For phones: manufacturer warranty covers defects only. If you are accident-prone, screen protection plans save money - screen repairs cost Rs. 5,000-20,000.
- For laptops: extended warranty is worth it on premium models (Rs. 80,000+) where repairs are expensive.
- Advice: check what our service covers - many repairs are cheaper than the plan.

## Refurbished vs new - what should I buy?
- Refurbished devices are only worth it when the savings exceed 40% and a warranty is included.
- We recommend buying new for phones and laptops - battery degradation in old devices is unavoidable.
- Advice: if budget is tight, buy a slightly older new model (like the Galaxy A15 5G) instead of a refurbished one.
