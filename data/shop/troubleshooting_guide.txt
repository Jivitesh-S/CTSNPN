# TechStore Troubleshooting Guide
Comprehensive step-by-step fixes for common phone, laptop and accessory problems.

# PHONES

## Phone won't charge
1. Use the original charger and cable that came with the phone.
2. Check the charging port for dust, lint, or debris and clean it gently with a dry toothpick or soft brush.
3. Try a different wall socket. Avoid charging through laptops or extension boards with heavy load.
4. Try wireless charging if your phone supports it.
5. Restart the phone and try again.
6. Check the cable for cuts or bends - damaged cables are the most common cause.
7. If the phone heats up while charging, remove the case and avoid using it while charging.
8. If none of these work, the charging port or battery may need service. Visit our store for a free diagnosis.

## Phone battery drains too fast
1. Check battery usage in Settings to find power-hungry apps.
2. Reduce screen brightness and turn off Always-On Display.
3. Enable power saving mode in Settings > Battery.
4. Turn off background app refresh for unused apps.
5. Disable Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and GPS when not in use.
6. Update the phone to the latest OS version.
7. Close apps you are not using, especially games and video streaming.
8. If the phone is more than 2 years old, the battery may need replacement. We replace batteries within 24 hours.

## Phone heats up / overheating
1. Remove the phone case to allow heat to escape.
2. Close all background apps, especially games, cameras, and navigation.
3. Lower screen brightness and avoid direct sunlight.
4. Stop charging while using heavy apps.
5. Avoid playing heavy games during charging.
6. Check for OS updates - overheating fixes are common in updates.
7. If overheating persists during light use, get a free diagnosis at our store. It may indicate a battery problem.

## Phone is slow / lagging
1. Restart the phone.
2. Clear cached data in Settings > Storage.
3. Delete unused apps, photos, and videos.
4. Check free storage - keep at least 15% of storage free.
5. Disable animations in Developer Options (Settings > About > Build number tapped 7 times).
6. Uninstall recently installed apps that may be causing issues.
7. Update the OS to the latest version.
8. If the phone still lags, back up data and do a factory reset.

## How to turn on phone / Phone won't turn on
1. Press and hold the Side / Power button on the right edge for 3-5 seconds until the logo appears.
2. If the phone does not power on, plug the phone into an official charger and wait 10-15 minutes - the battery may be completely drained.
3. Force Restart: If the screen is black and unresponsive, press and hold both the Volume Down and Power buttons simultaneously for 10-15 seconds until the device vibrates and reboots.
4. Check the charging cable and adapter on another socket.
5. Connect to a PC or laptop - if the phone vibrates or is detected, the display may need service.
6. If the device still does not turn on, visit TechStore (+91 9087086182) for free diagnostics.


## Phone screen is cracked or damaged
1. Stop using the phone if glass is broken - small shards can cut fingers.
2. Back up your data using cloud backup (Google Drive/iCloud) while the screen is still usable.
3. Do not attempt DIY screen repairs - it voids warranty and can damage the phone.
4. Visit our store - screen replacement for popular models takes 48 hours.
5. After repair, apply a tempered glass protector.

## Phone touch screen not responding
1. Remove the screen protector - it may be interfering.
2. Clean the screen with a soft dry cloth.
3. Restart the phone.
4. Check for water or moisture on the screen.
5. In Settings > Accessibility, check touch sensitivity settings.
6. If the issue persists, the digitizer may be faulty - screen replacement is needed.

## Phone camera not working / black screen
1. Close the camera app completely and reopen it.
2. Restart the phone.
3. Check if other apps can use the camera - if not, the camera hardware may be blocked by another app.
4. Clear camera app data in Settings > Apps.
5. Update the phone software.
6. If the camera lens is dirty, clean gently with a microfiber cloth.
7. If the problem persists, the camera module needs service.

## Phone speakers not working / no sound
1. Check that the volume is turned up and the phone is not on silent.
2. Check if sound works through headphones - if yes, the speaker is the problem.
3. Clean the speaker grille gently with a soft dry brush.
4. Restart the phone.
5. Disable Bluetooth to rule out a connected device stealing audio.
6. Check the media volume separately from the ring volume.
7. If no sound at all, the speaker module needs replacement - typically fixed within 24 hours.

## Phone microphone not working
1. Check if the problem is in calls only or all recordings.
2. Remove the phone case - it may be covering the mic.
3. Clean the microphone hole with a soft brush - lint is a common cause.
4. Restart the phone.
5. Check that no app is blocking microphone access in Settings > Privacy.
6. If calls fail but recordings work, check network/call settings with your carrier.
7. If the mic is faulty, visit our store for service.

## Phone Wi-Fi not connecting
1. Turn off Wi-Fi and turn it back on.
2. Restart the router and the phone.
3. Forget the network (Settings > Wi-Fi > long press > Forget) and reconnect with the password.
4. Make sure airplane mode is off.
5. Check that the router allows multiple devices.
6. Reset network settings (Settings > General Management > Reset > Reset Network Settings).
7. If only one network fails but others work, the issue is with that router.

## Phone Bluetooth not pairing
1. Turn Bluetooth off and on again.
2. Put the accessory in pairing mode (check its manual).
3. Delete the device from the paired list and pair again.
4. Make sure the devices are within 1 metre of each other.
5. Restart both devices.
6. Reset network settings on the phone.
7. Check battery levels on both devices.

## Phone keeps restarting / boot loop
1. Force restart by holding the power button for 20 seconds.
2. Charge the phone for at least 30 minutes and retry.
3. Try booting into Safe Mode (hold power, then long-press 'Power off' and confirm Safe Mode).
4. If Safe Mode works, uninstall recently installed apps.
5. Update the operating system.
6. If boot loop persists, back up data via recovery mode and factory reset.
7. Visit our store for a free diagnosis - this can indicate a motherboard or battery issue.

## Phone fell in water
1. Remove the phone from water immediately.
2. Turn the phone OFF immediately - do not press buttons.
3. Do NOT charge it, do NOT use a hair dryer, do NOT put it in rice.
4. Wipe the exterior gently with a dry cloth.
5. Remove the SIM tray and case.
6. Bring the phone to our service centre immediately - the faster it is cleaned internally, the higher the chance of saving it.
7. Do not power it on until our technician checks it.

## Phone storage full
1. Back up photos and videos to Google Photos or iCloud.
2. Clear app caches in Settings > Storage.
3. Delete duplicate and unwanted files.
4. Move large files to a microSD card (if supported) or to cloud storage.
5. Uninstall apps you no longer use.
6. Use the phone's built-in storage cleaner tool.

## Phone not receiving calls / calls dropping
1. Check that the SIM is inserted properly.
2. Check signal strength - move to an open area.
3. Turn airplane mode on and off to refresh network registration.
4. Check call forwarding settings in the dialer.
5. Remove and reinsert the SIM card.
6. Update the phone software.
7. If the problem continues, test the SIM in another phone - if it works, the issue is the phone; if not, contact your carrier.

## Android phone apps keep crashing
1. Clear the app's cache (Settings > Apps > App > Clear Cache).
2. Clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > App > Clear Data) - note this logs you out.
3. Update the app from the Play Store.
4. Restart the phone.
5. Check for OS updates.
6. Uninstall and reinstall the app.
7. Check storage - low storage causes crashes.

## Galaxy phone apps keep crashing
1. Force close the app by swiping it away in the Recents view.
2. Restart the Galaxy phone.
3. Update the app from the Galaxy Store or Play Store.
4. Update One UI in Settings > Software Update.
5. Clear the app's cache (Settings > Apps > App > Storage > Clear Cache) and try again.
6. Check available storage - low storage causes crashes.

## Phone fingerprint sensor not working
1. Make sure fingers are clean and dry.
2. Remove and re-add fingerprints in Settings.
3. Remove the screen protector if it is thick.
4. Restart the phone.
5. If the sensor is physical (side or rear), clean it gently.
6. If the issue persists, the sensor needs service.

## Phone face unlock not working
1. Make sure the front camera is clean.
2. Improve lighting - face unlock struggles in very dark or very bright conditions.
3. Re-register the face in Settings.
4. Remove glasses or masks during registration and use.
5. If using a screen protector with a cutout, ensure it is not covering the camera.

## Phone not detected by PC / laptop
1. Unlock the phone before connecting.
2. On Android, pull down the notification shade and select 'File Transfer' or 'MTP' mode.
3. Try a different USB cable and port.
4. Install/update the USB drivers on the PC.
5. On Galaxy, unlock and tap 'Trust This Computer' / 'Allow' for USB debugging when prompted.
6. Restart both devices.

# LAPTOPS

## Laptop not turning on / Laptop won't boot / no display
1. Check the power adapter and charging LED indicator light. Let the laptop charge for 15-20 minutes.
2. Hard Power Reset: Disconnect all USB devices and the charger. Press and hold the power button for 20-30 seconds continuously to drain static power, then reconnect charger and press power once.
3. If fans or keyboard lights turn on but the screen is black, press Win + Ctrl + Shift + B to reset display drivers.
4. Connect an external monitor to check if the laptop screen or video cable is faulty.
5. Enter the BIOS (press F2, F10, or Del during boot) - if BIOS loads, the issue is Windows/OS related.
6. If the laptop shows no power lights and does not respond, bring it to TechStore (+91 9087086182) for free diagnostics.


## Laptop is very slow
1. Restart the laptop.
2. Check which apps are consuming resources in Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc).
3. Close background programs and browser tabs.
4. Check free disk space - keep at least 20% free.
5. Disable startup programs (Task Manager > Startup).
6. Scan for viruses and malware.
7. Update Windows and device drivers.
8. Upgrade to an SSD if the laptop has a hard drive - this is the single biggest speed upgrade.
9. Upgrade RAM to 16GB if the laptop has 8GB or less.

## Laptop overheating
1. Place the laptop on a hard flat surface - never on a bed or pillow.
2. Clean the vents with compressed air - dust is the #1 cause of overheating.
3. Use a cooling pad for intensive work.
4. Check that the fans spin - if silent while hot, fans may be failing.
5. Update the BIOS and graphics drivers.
6. Lower performance settings or limit background tasks.
7. Repaste the CPU if the laptop is 2+ years old and running hot.
8. If it overheats and shuts down, visit our store for cleaning and thermal service.

## Laptop battery draining fast
1. Check battery health (Settings > System > Power > Battery Health or manufacturer tool).
2. Lower screen brightness.
3. Enable Battery Saver mode.
4. Disable keyboard backlight when not needed.
5. Close GPU-heavy apps (games, video editing).
6. Check for battery-draining background apps in Task Manager.
7. Disconnect unused USB devices.
8. If the battery health is below 80%, a replacement is recommended - we replace laptop batteries within 24 hours.

## Laptop keyboard not working
1. Restart the laptop - keyboard issues are often temporary.
2. Check if it is a hardware or software issue by opening an on-screen keyboard.
3. On Windows, run the keyboard troubleshooter (Settings > System > Troubleshoot).
4. Check for stuck keys and debris - clean gently with compressed air.
5. Connect an external keyboard - if it works, the built-in keyboard may be faulty.
6. Check if a recent driver update caused it - roll back the keyboard driver.
7. If the keys are unresponsive, the keyboard needs replacement.

## Laptop touchpad not working
1. Check if the touchpad is disabled - most laptops toggle it with F6, F9, or Fn+F keys.
2. Restart the laptop.
3. Check touchpad settings (Settings > Bluetooth & Devices > Touchpad).
4. Update the touchpad driver in Device Manager.
5. Disconnect an external mouse and test again.
6. If the touchpad still doesn't work, it may need service.

## Laptop screen flickering
1. Update the graphics driver.
2. Check refresh rate settings (Settings > System > Display > Advanced Display).
3. Lower the refresh rate temporarily to test.
4. Disable hardware acceleration in the browser.
5. Run the display troubleshooter.
6. If flickering continues, the display cable or panel may be faulty.

## Laptop Wi-Fi not working
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on (or Fn+F key toggle).
2. Restart the router and laptop.
3. Update the Wi-Fi driver in Device Manager.
4. Check if the Wi-Fi adapter is disabled in Device Manager.
5. Run Windows network troubleshooter.
6. Reset network settings (Settings > Network > Advanced > Network Reset).
7. If the adapter shows an error, the hardware may need service.

## Laptop audio not working
1. Check the volume and mute state.
2. Check the default output device (right-click the speaker icon > Sound Settings).
3. Ensure the correct output device is selected (speakers vs headphones).
4. Run the audio troubleshooter.
5. Update the audio driver.
6. Restart the Windows Audio service (services.msc > Windows Audio > Restart).
7. Test with headphones - if they work, the internal speakers may be faulty.

## Laptop stuck on update screen
1. Wait 30-60 minutes - Windows updates can take a long time.
2. If stuck for hours, force shutdown by holding the power button 15 seconds.
3. On restart, Windows usually resumes or rolls back the update.
4. If it keeps failing, boot into Safe Mode and use Windows Update Troubleshooter.
5. Free up disk space - updates need 20GB+ free.
6. Run DISM and SFC repair commands in an admin command prompt: sfc /scannow and DISM /Online /Cleanup-Image /RestoreHealth.

## Laptop makes loud fan noise
1. Close heavy applications.
2. Check Task Manager for high CPU usage.
3. Clean the vents with compressed air.
4. Update the BIOS - fan curves are often improved.
5. Use 'Balanced' power mode instead of 'Best Performance'.
6. If the fan rattles or runs at full speed constantly, the fan may need replacement.

## Laptop USB ports not working
1. Restart the laptop.
2. Try the device on a different port.
3. Update USB drivers (Device Manager > Universal Serial Bus controllers).
4. Check Device Manager for yellow warning icons.
5. Disable and re-enable the USB controller.
6. Check for debris in the port - clean gently with a toothpick.
7. If all ports fail, the motherboard chip may need service.

## Laptop shows blue screen (BSOD)
1. Note the error code on the screen.
2. Restart the laptop.
3. Remove recently installed hardware and uninstall recently installed software.
4. Run sfc /scannow in an admin command prompt.
5. Update drivers, especially graphics and chipset.
6. Check RAM with Windows Memory Diagnostic (mdsched.exe).
7. Boot into Safe Mode - if it works there, a driver or app is the cause.
8. If blue screens continue, visit our store for diagnosis.

## Laptop hard drive/SSD full
1. Run Disk Cleanup (search 'Disk Cleanup' and select the drive).
2. Clean temporary files (Settings > System > Storage > Temporary Files).
3. Uninstall unused software.
4. Move personal files to an external drive or cloud.
5. Check for large files with a disk space analyzer.
6. Upgrade to a larger SSD - we install and clone drives in store.

## Laptop webcam not working
1. Check the privacy shutter (physical cover) - many laptops have one.
2. Check camera privacy settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera).
3. Check if the app has camera permission.
4. Update the camera driver.
5. Press Fn+F8 or similar key if the camera has a kill switch.
6. Test in another app to isolate the issue.

# ACCESSORIES

## TWS earbuds not pairing
1. Remove the earbuds from the case - they enter pairing mode automatically.
2. If not, press and hold the touch area for 5-10 seconds.
3. Delete the device from your phone's Bluetooth list and pair fresh.
4. Make sure the earbuds are charged.
5. Reset the earbuds (check the manual - usually 3 taps + hold on both buds).
6. Keep the phone within 1 metre during pairing.
7. If only one bud plays sound, place both buds back in the case and take them out together.

## TWS earbuds one side not working
1. Place both earbuds in the case and close it for 10 seconds, then remove them together.
2. Check that both buds show charge in the case.
3. Clean the charging contacts on the buds and case with a dry cloth.
4. Delete and re-pair the earbuds.
5. Check the balance setting on the phone (Settings > Accessibility > Audio Balance).
6. If one bud still fails, it may need service under warranty.

## Earbuds low volume
1. Check the volume on both the phone and the earbuds.
2. Check for earwax blocking the speaker mesh - clean gently.
3. Check the audio balance setting.
4. Some earbuds have a volume limit feature - check the companion app.
5. Make sure the earbuds are fully charged.

## Headphones not charging
1. Check the charging cable and port - clean the port gently.
2. Use the original cable.
3. Leave it charging for 30 minutes even if no light appears.
4. Try a different charger.
5. If the headphone has a replaceable battery compartment, check battery orientation.
6. If still not charging, the battery may need service.

## Bluetooth speaker not connecting
1. Put the speaker in pairing mode (usually long-press the Bluetooth button).
2. Delete it from the phone and pair fresh.
3. Check the speaker battery level.
4. Move the speaker closer to the phone.
5. Reset the speaker (check manual for reset combo).
6. Try pairing with another phone to isolate the issue.

## Bluetooth speaker has low sound or distortion
1. Check the phone volume and speaker volume separately.
2. Lower the volume - distortion at max volume is normal for small speakers.
3. Check that no EQ is boosting bass heavily.
4. Update the speaker firmware via its app if available.
5. Clean the speaker grille gently.

## Power bank not charging phone
1. Check the cable first - swap with another cable.
2. Press the power button on the power bank to wake it up.
3. Check the power bank's own charge level.
4. Use the right output port (some are for low-power devices only).
5. Check that your phone's cable is properly inserted.
6. If the power bank won't charge itself, try another charger and cable.
7. If it still fails, the power bank battery may be dead - check warranty.

## Power bank charges slowly
1. Use a higher wattage cable and charger.
2. Make sure the power bank supports fast charging (check specs).
3. Charging two devices at once halves the speed per device.
4. Some phones need a specific port for fast charging.
5. Check that the cable supports fast charging (look for 5A rated cables).

## Wireless mouse not working
1. Check the battery and replace if low.
2. Reconnect the USB receiver - try a different port.
3. Press the connect button on the mouse and receiver to re-pair.
4. Clean the mouse sensor with a dry cloth.
5. Check the surface - glass and glossy surfaces confuse optical sensors.
6. Restart the PC or reinstall the driver.

## Router Wi-Fi keeps dropping
1. Restart the router (unplug 30 seconds, plug back in).
2. Update the router firmware.
3. Move the router to a central, elevated location away from walls.
4. Change the Wi-Fi channel in router settings (try 1, 6, or 11 on 2.4GHz).
5. Check for interference from microwaves, baby monitors, and other routers.
6. Limit connected devices - too many devices overload cheap routers.
7. Check the router's heat - routers overheat in closed cabinets.

## Router slow internet speed
1. Restart the router and modem.
2. Test speed wired vs wireless - if wired is fast but wireless slow, it's a Wi-Fi issue.
3. Change the band to 5GHz for newer devices.
4. Update router firmware.
5. Check for bandwidth hogs (background downloads, cloud sync).
6. Contact your ISP if wired speeds are also slow.

# SMART TELEVISIONS & MONITORS

## Samsung TV won't turn on / Red light blinking
1. Cold Boot / Power Reset: Unplug the TV power cord from the wall outlet. Press and hold the physical Power button on the TV chassis (bottom or rear) for 30 seconds to drain residual power. Wait 2 minutes, then plug directly into the wall (avoid extension sockets) and power on.
2. Check the Red Standby Light:
   - If red light is solid OFF: Check the power socket or test with another appliance.
   - If red light is blinking continuously: Count the blinks (usually indicates power board or backlight protection).
3. If using a One Connect Box (on Frame / Neo QLED / OLED S95D), ensure the invisible silver fiber cable is firmly seated at both ends without sharp bends.
4. Try powering on using the physical power button on the TV frame to rule out dead remote batteries.
5. If the red light stays blinking or no power returns, contact TechStore (+91 9087086182) for an in-home technician visit.

## Samsung TV black screen with sound (No picture)
1. Flashlight Test: Shine a smartphone flashlight closely at the dark screen. If you can see faint images or menu outlines, the display panel is fine but the LED backlight / power inverter needs service.
2. Picture Test: On your remote, press Home > Settings > Support > Device Care > Self Diagnosis > Picture Test.
3. Check HDMI cables: Disconnect all HDMI devices (set-top box, gaming console, firestick). If the TV menu/home screen appears normally, the issue is with the external device or HDMI cable.
4. Firmware update: Update Tizen OS to latest version via Settings > Support > Software Update.
5. If picture test fails or screen remains pitch black, book a free technician inspection with TechStore.

## Samsung TV HDMI eARC / Soundbar audio not working
1. Use a High-Speed HDMI 2.1 cable certified for eARC and connect to the dedicated HDMI Port labeled "eARC" (usually HDMI Port 3).
2. Enable Anynet+ (HDMI-CEC): Navigate to Settings > General > External Device Manager > Anynet+ (HDMI-CEC) and turn it ON.
3. Configure Sound Output: Go to Settings > Sound > Expert Settings > Digital Output Audio Format and select "Pass-Through" or "Auto".
4. Set eARC Mode to "Auto" in Sound Expert Settings.
5. Power cycle the TV and Soundbar simultaneously (unplug both for 60 seconds).

## Samsung TV Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting / Tizen OS network error
1. Restart the TV via remote: Press and hold the Remote Power button for 5 seconds until the Samsung logo appears (soft reboot).
2. DNS Settings Fix: Go to Settings > General > Network > Network Status > IP Settings > DNS Setting > Set to "Enter Manually" > DNS Server: 8.8.8.8 (Google DNS).
3. Forget and Reconnect Wi-Fi: Delete the saved Wi-Fi network, restart your home router, and reconnect entering your password fresh.
4. Date & Time Sync: Ensure Settings > General > System Manager > Time > Clock is set to Auto (incorrect time prevents SSL connections for YouTube/Netflix).

## Samsung SolarCell Remote not working / Unresponsive
1. Solar / USB-C Charging: Place the remote face-down under direct sunlight or indoor lighting, or plug a USB Type-C cable into the bottom port for 30 minutes.
2. Re-pair Remote: Stand 1 foot away from the TV, point at the sensor, and press and hold the "Return" (Back) and "Play/Pause" buttons simultaneously for 3 seconds until "Pairing Device..." appears on screen.
3. Clean the IR emitter lens at the top of the remote with a soft microfiber cloth.

# WASHING MACHINES & DRYERS

## Samsung Washing Machine 4C / 4E Error Code (Water Supply Issue)
- Meaning: Water is not entering the drum or filling too slowly.
- Step 1: Check water tap - ensure the inlet tap connected to the machine is fully OPEN.
- Step 2: Check inlet hose - verify the water hose is not kinked, twisted, or crushed against the wall.
- Step 3: Clean Debris Mesh Filter - Turn off the tap, unscrew the L-shaped water inlet hose at the back of the machine, remove the small plastic mesh filter with pliers, rinse out lime/sand debris under running water, and reconnect firmly.
- Step 4: Check water pressure - Ensure minimum household water pressure is at least 0.5 bar (50 kPa).

## Samsung Washing Machine 5C / 5E / Nd Error Code (Drainage Issue)
- Meaning: Water is unable to drain out of the drum during rinse/spin cycles.
- Step 1: Clean Emergency Drain Filter (Pump Filter): Open the small access flap at the bottom-right front of the machine. Place a shallow tray and towel underneath. Pull the small rubber drain tube to release excess water into the tray. Unscrew the circular debris filter counter-clockwise, remove coins, lint, hairpins, and clean under warm water. Screw back tightly.
- Step 2: Check drain hose height: Ensure the drain hose is elevated between 60cm to 90cm from the floor (not pushed too deep into the standpipe or lying flat on ground).
- Step 3: Check for frozen or kinked drain pipe.

## Samsung Washing Machine Ub / UE Error Code (Unbalanced Load)
- Meaning: The drum cannot reach high-speed spin due to uneven weight distribution.
- Step 1: Redistribute clothes: Open the door and evenly spread out bulky items (bedsheets, heavy jackets, towels). Avoid washing a single heavy item alone — add 1-2 towels to balance the drum.
- Step 2: Level the machine: Check that all four leveling feet touch the floor firmly. Use a spirit level on top of the machine and adjust the lock nuts underneath with a spanner.
- Step 3: Run Calibration Mode: Empty the drum, turn machine off, press and hold "Temp" + "Delay End" simultaneously, then press Power. Press Start to run the 3-minute auto-calibration cycle (CLB).

## Samsung Washing Machine dC / dE / dE1 Error Code (Door Lock Fault)
- Meaning: The door is open, not latched, or laundry is trapped in the rubber gasket.
- Step 1: Ensure no garments or socks are caught between the glass door and rubber seal.
- Step 2: Push the door firmly until you hear a distinct mechanical click.
- Step 3: If child lock is active, press and hold the Child Lock buttons (usually Temp + Rinse) for 3 seconds to unlock.

## Samsung Washing Machine AC6 / 3C / 3E Error Code (Motor / Communication Issue)
- Meaning: Digital Inverter Motor overload or main control board communication glitch.
- Step 1: Power cycle: Switch off the wall power switch and unplug the machine for 15 minutes to reset the inverter drive module.
- Step 2: Reduce load: If the drum was overloaded with heavy wet duvets, remove half the items.
- Step 3: If the error code returns immediately upon pressing Start, contact TechStore (+91 9087086182) for a certified technician motor inspection (Digital Inverter Motor is backed by a 20-year warranty).

## Samsung EcoBubble Not Foaming / High Vibration
1. Water Temperature: EcoBubble works with cold water (15°C–25°C). Ensure you are not running boiling hot water directly into the bubble generator.
2. Detergent Type: Use liquid Front-Load detergent (Matik) in the correct auto-dispenser compartment. Do not use standard hand-wash powder which causes excessive residue.
3. Transit Bolts: If the machine is brand new and vibrating violently across the floor, ensure the 4 rear metal transit bolts (shipping safety bolts) were removed during installation!
